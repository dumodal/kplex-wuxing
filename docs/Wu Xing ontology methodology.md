# Wu Xing ontology methodology

## Core principle

**For this experimental fork, Wuxing is understood primarily through process, change, and quality.** The phase names direct attention toward characteristic ways of moving, developing, transforming, and relating. Their meaning extends beyond the physical materials that their names also designate.

A phase therefore asks us to attend to what is happening, how it is changing, and the quality of that change. This is the methodological foundation for the ontology and its navigation system.

## Wood as the worked example

The passage that prompted this note describes the post-heaven arrangement of Wuxing as "primarily concerned with process, change, and quality." Its example associates Wood with the "vital essence" and growth of trees, rather than reducing the phase to wood as a physical substance. The same passage acknowledges that material meanings remain relevant in some contexts. [Source: Wikipedia, Etymology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuxing_(Chinese_philosophy)#Etymology).

In the phenomenological reading proposed for this project, a tree offers an encounter with growth: a shoot emerging, branches extending, roots taking hold, and a living form responding to its surroundings. The perceived qualities of these processes help articulate what Wood means in the framework.

The material object provides an experiential point of entry. Attention then turns toward its activity and becoming. Seeing a tree, handling a wooden object, and noticing the unfolding growth of a tree are different encounters; naming a material alone does not exhaust the phase.

Here, "phenomenological" names attention to how a process is experienced and perceived. It records the personal interpretive approach behind this fork; it does not attribute a particular modern school of phenomenology to all historical Wuxing traditions.

## Cycle order and situated reference

The general introduction presents generation as Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood. This explanatory order follows the generating sequence; it does not make Wood a permanent centre or give the cycle an absolute beginning. The reference for a reading can be any phase. [Source: Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/wuxing/).

Wood supplies the default teaching reference through the digital garden example below. A personal reference can differ from that teaching default; it does not change the general generating sequence.

## The digital garden as an entry into the ontology

The current introductory example starts with Wood (木, mù). The digital garden provides an accessible image of growth, branching connections, and possibilities developing over time. It is a teaching metaphor chosen for this framework, not a claim that every digital garden or every note has one fixed phase.

The learning process begins with a person understanding a unique relationship with information: what they remember, what they encounter online, what they gather, what they create, and how those materials change through use. "I" is that participant. Wood is the initial reference for exploring the practice; it is not a universal identity imposed on the person.

A memory and a captured web page can both participate in this garden. Their origin offers an entry into interpretation, while their present role and direction guide classification. Original expression may begin as 我生 and outside capture as 我克, according to the accepted A/B defaults. Reworking, sharing, returning to, or setting aside material can change its role. The method is to attend to these relationships rather than assigning phases from file type, subject matter, or the garden image alone.

The framework should help the reader encounter and work with information. Onboarding therefore moves from the person and the garden to a reference phase, then to concrete relationships and existing notes. It does not require the reader to study the ten Heavenly Stems or calculate a birth chart before beginning. The current guide starts with Wood; other phase references remain available for exploration.

### Optional personal background: the Metal reference

In the practice of dumo, the personal Metal reference comes from the user-confirmed 辛金 (xīn jīn, Yin Metal) Day Master. In BaZi, the Day Master is the Heavenly Stem of the birth day and acts as the reference for a chart reading. This is traditional symbolic context for the personal design, distinct from a claim that the graph measures a birth-derived characteristic. [Practitioner reference: Master Mandy Lim](https://masterlimfengshui.com/info/learn/day-master/).

BaZi is optional in this adaptation. The Metal example can serve as a later tutorial comparison: from Wood, original expression starts as Fire and outside capture as Earth; from Metal, those defaults become Water and Wood. The change illustrates how a reference changes the relationships. It does not make the Metal example a default, add a Heavenly Stem property to notes, or assign yin-yang polarity. The current introductory classification remains at the five-phase level.

For graph navigation, the selected note becomes a temporary reference in the implemented phase model. It does not become the person or replace the personal reference used to assign statuses. The interpretation of rotating note-to-note relationships remains a separate ontology question, recorded in [[Wu Xing terminology]].

## Proposed extension: learning through classification

A further proposal makes the digital garden an ongoing practice inside navigation. Existing hyperlinks supply the neighborhood. Unclassified connected notes would remain visible in the middle of the pentagon, and deliberate movement into a phase would become a metadata assignment. This would let understanding develop through working with actual notes, rather than requiring a complete classification before the view becomes useful.

Preserve the saved phase of a current note. The user approved Wood as a default only for an unclassified current note; opening an already classified note must not reset it to Wood. Whether that initial Wood default is provisional or explicitly saved remains an implementation question. A provisional reference for learning and a saved interpretation of a note are distinct states.

The central holding area would mean unclassified, not a sixth phase, a neutral relationship family, or an absence of meaning. Phase assignment would still depend on the role of the material in the practice. Gathering notes in the centre must not create new relationships. This is a proposed extension for upstream discussion; the current fork only reads statuses. [[Wu Xing onboarding content]] contains the short overview and the prompt for Zsolt.

## Three questions for interpretation

| Dimension | Guiding question | Application to notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Process | What is happening here? | Notice the activity in which the note participates: expression, engagement, connection, selection, manifestation, or integration. |
| Change | What is becoming different? | Attend to the movement from captured material to interpretation, from interpretation to expression, or from action to reflection. |
| Quality | How does this movement unfold? | Consider its felt and relational character: opening, branching, clarifying, intensifying, settling, or another quality named in context. |

These questions are methodological prompts for this adaptation. They are not a universal dictionary assigning each verb to one fixed phase.

## Consequences for the ontology

1. **Assign phases through activity and context.** A note about a tree does not acquire Wood status merely because of its subject. A note written on paper does not acquire Wood status merely because of its carrier. Consider its role in the current practice.
2. **Allow activities to change role.** Writing, reading, drawing, or collecting can participate in different phases. A phase assignment records a contextual interpretation that can be revisited as the material is used or transformed.
3. **Read relationships from a reference point.** The five families describe relations relative to "I." For personal status assignment, this is the participant organizing the practice. For graph navigation, the selected note becomes the temporary reference for reading phase relationships. Moving through the graph does not itself rewrite status.
4. **Use A/B as starting defaults.** Material originating in personal expression begins as 我生, "What I generate." Material captured from outside begins as 我克, "What I obtain" in the capture workflow. These entry defaults remain open to transformation and reinterpretation. Their phase statuses depend on the chosen reference phase.
5. **Keep the model reading and recorded contribution distinguishable.** A phase-derived relationship expresses the model. A source reference records a particular connection. Whether and how the latter should receive an explicit relationship family remains an ontology decision; the process principle alone does not settle it.
6. **Explain qualities before presenting the diagram.** Onboarding should introduce process, change, and quality, then the reference point and the phase names. Colors and positions help navigate that interpretation. They do not supply its meaning on their own.

Existing statuses in dumodal remain the prepared foundation for the current test. This note does not request retrospective relabeling or an automatic metadata migration.

## Source grounding and project interpretation

The Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy describes the phases as dynamic and interdependent, discusses movement and transformation in the meaning of xing, and explains why different contexts can support different translations. This supports a process-oriented introduction while leaving room for the historical range of uses. [Source: Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/wuxing/).

The stronger design commitment in this note is specific to this project: approach the phases through lived qualities of activity and change, then use that reading to develop the note ontology. The tree example, the phenomenological emphasis, and the A/B intake defaults are the interpretive method being developed here. They should not be presented as an exhaustive historical definition of Wuxing or as measurements supplied by the graph.

The project now uses "Five Phases" as its working English name. The introduction retains the original term Wu Xing (五行), with pinyin wǔ xíng in a side note. "Five Agents" remains a possible future translation pending feedback. The current term should retain the process-oriented meaning described here when explaining the framework to new users.

## Direction and the cost of excess

The current working reading of 克我 concerns direction and rules acting on the reference point. They may guide without nourishing, and excess can restrict or exhaust. The reading of 我克 concerns what the reference point obtains from outside or brings under its influence. Obtaining something does not require continued attention to it; excessive capture or management consumes time and energy. These are distinct directions with distinct effects in the personal framework.
